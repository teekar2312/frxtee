   476→   476→- L76 `"sharpe": 1.4` — **hardcoded constant**, not computed from returns. `backtest-view.tsx:120` displays it as if measured. Misleading.
   477→   477→- L70 `pf = gross_win / gross_loss if gross_loss else gross_win` — when gross_loss==0, PF = gross_win (inflated). Should be `inf` or `None`.
   478→   478→- L78-79 `expectancy` formula uses `gross_win / max(wins,1)` (avg win) but multiplies by `win_rate/100` and subtracts `(1 - win_rate/100) * (gross_loss/max(losses,1))` — mathematically dubious, doesn't equal mean per-trade PnL.
   479→   479→- Fix: subtract spread + pip slippage on entry and exit; subtract `$1 * lot` commission; compute Sharpe from per-trade returns; compute expectancy as `mean(pnls)`; compute PF as `sum(wins)/abs(sum(losses))` with explicit handling of zero losses.
   480→   480→
   481→   481→### 8. Look-ahead bias in backtest — PASS (no leak), with caveat
   482→   482→File: `python-backend/backtest.py:17-34`
   483→   483→- L17-21: indicators (ema, rsi, macd) computed on the **full df** before the loop.
   484→   484→  - `ema` = `ewm(span, adjust=False)` — recursive backward, causal. ✓
   485→   485→  - `rsi` = `rolling(period).mean()` of gains/losses — past `period` bars only. ✓
   486→   486→  - `macd` = ewm differences — causal. ✓
   487→   487→  - No future-leak from indicator computation.
   488→   488→- L34-42: loop accesses `df.iloc[i]` (current bar) and `df.iloc[i + 5]` (5 bars forward) — the i+5 access is the trade's exit price, which is legitimate (you exit 5 bars later).
   489→   489→- Caveat (LOW): computing indicators on the entire `df` rather than incrementally means EMA's warmup state at bar i is the same as it would be live (because ewm is recursive), but `rolling().std()` of pct_change for `vol_5` (in ml_model.py, not backtest) uses sample statistics that are identical live vs. backtest. No leak.
   490→   490→- Caveat (LOW): backtest signals at bar i use indicators at bar i, then enters at `row["close"]` of bar i — in live trading you'd enter at bar i+1 open (next-bar open after signal confirmation). Mild optimistic bias (~1 bar slippage equivalent), not look-ahead per se.
   491→   491→- **No look-ahead bias.** Mark PASS.
