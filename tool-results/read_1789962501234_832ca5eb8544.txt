   470→### 7. Backtest realism — HIGH (idealized, overstated)
   471→File: `python-backend/backtest.py:34-46`
   472→- L41 `entry = row["close"]` — entry is at the close of the signal bar (no slippage, no spread).
   473→- L42 `exit_ = df.iloc[i + 5]["close"]` — exit at close of bar+5 (no slippage, no spread).
   474→- L43 `pips = (exit_ - entry) / pip` — raw price delta, **no spread subtracted, no commission, no slippage.** FINEX spec (per `trading-data.ts`) is 0.5 pip spread + $1/lot commission — neither appears.
   475→- L45 `pnl = pips * ps.lot * 10` — hardcoded `$10/pip/lot`. Wrong for XAUUSD (where 1 lot = 100 oz and a "pip"=0.1 → $10/pip/lot actually coincidentally works) and XAGUSD (where 1 lot = 5000 oz and pip=0.01 → $50/pip/lot, NOT $10). Multiplies the error further for JPY pairs.
   476→- L76 `"sharpe": 1.4` — **hardcoded constant**, not computed from returns. `backtest-view.tsx:120` displays it as if measured. Misleading.
   477→- L70 `pf = gross_win / gross_loss if gross_loss else gross_win` — when gross_loss==0, PF = gross_win (inflated). Should be `inf` or `None`.
   478→- L78-79 `expectancy` formula uses `gross_win / max(wins,1)` (avg win) but multiplies by `win_rate/100` and subtracts `(1 - win_rate/100) * (gross_loss/max(losses,1))` — mathematically dubious, doesn't equal mean per-trade PnL.
   479→- Fix: subtract spread + pip slippage on entry and exit; subtract `$1 * lot` commission; compute Sharpe from per-trade returns; compute expectancy as `mean(pnls)`; compute PF as `sum(wins)/abs(sum(losses))` with explicit handling of zero losses.

   481→### 8. Look-ahead bias in backtest — PASS (no leak), with caveat

   485→  - `rsi` = `rolling(period).mean()` of gains/losses — past `period` bars only. ✓
   486→  - `macd` = ewm differences — causal. ✓
